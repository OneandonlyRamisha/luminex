## 2. Positioning

### Category we compete in

**Not:** sunglasses, fashion eyewear, reading glasses.
**Yes:** performance wearables — same shelf as Oura, Whoop, Eight Sleep, Bryan Johnson's Blueprint. Tool category, not accessory category.

### Positioning statement

> For operators, creators, athletes, and knowledge workers who treat sleep and focus as trainable, Luminex is a pair of Georgian-made tinted lenses calibrated to specific circadian states. Unlike fashion sunglasses, each model is engineered around a measured spectral profile — verified by independent optics labs — and named for a Georgian figure whose life's work was light.

### What we are

- **Instrument-grade.** Lab-certified. Numbers on the page.
- **Circadian-aware.** Each model is tied to a _time of day_ and a _physiological state_.
- **Georgian.** Named, made, and shipped from Tbilisi. The heritage is load-bearing, not decorative.
- **Direct.** No retailers, no middlemen, no markup. 74 ₾ is the real price.

### What we are not

- Not luxury jewelry.
- Not medical devices (we don't treat disease).
- Not generic blue-blockers (we publish the actual transmission spectra).
- Not trend-driven (a Luminex model should look the same in 2030).

### Customer

- **Primary:** 24–40, Tbilisi/Batumi/Kutaisi, spends >6 hrs/day on a screen, cares about sleep quality, probably owns a smart ring or tracks training, buys online.
- **Secondary:** Georgian diaspora (EU, US) wanting a premium product _from home_.
- **Reach:** anyone globally who finds us via wellness/biohacking channels — our pricing is a wedge.

### Price rationale

74 ₾ sits 2–3× above generic Georgian market eyewear (20–40 ₾) and well below global wellness optics ($60–200+). It reads **premium-local, accessible-global** — precisely where a Tbilisi-born performance brand should enter. The number is fixed across the line. No tier inflation.

## 3. Voice & Tone

### Do

1. **Speak in tool language.** _Calibrated. Tuned. Transmission. Spectrum. Filter. Instrument._ We earn these words because we have lab data.
2. **Name the figure briefly, let their life carry the weight.** One sentence of biography, maximum. The reader Googles the rest.
3. **Quote numbers when they do work.** "13% transmission in the 475–650 nm band" beats "very dark." Use sparingly — when a number replaces an adjective.
4. **Short sentences. Verbs in front.** The voice is closer to a rifle scope manual than a perfume ad.

### Don't

1. **No fashion vocabulary.** Stylish, chic, trendy, statement, iconic — strike on sight.
2. **No wellness clichés.** Self-care, treat yourself, indulge, glow up, vibes.
3. **No over-explaining the Georgian angle.** The names do the work. Don't apologize for them, don't translate every reference, don't lecture.
4. **No emoji in body copy.** Product names can carry a color dot (🔴🟡🟠) as a spec marker only.

- **Banned:** stock smiles, white teeth, pool deck shots, "lifestyle" staged groups.

- Inside: cloth pouch (unbleached linen), microfiber cleaning cloth, **a printed lab certification card** specific to the model purchased — this is core to the brand, not optional.

## 6. 01 · PIROSMANI 🔴

### Model code

`LMX-01-PIR-RED`

### One-line

**The lens for the painter's hour.** Wear after sundown to protect sleep.

### The name

**Niko Pirosmani** (1862–1918) was a self-taught Tbilisi painter who spent his life in the city's night. He painted tavern signs for bread, slept in cellars, and captured a world lit by oil lamps and moonlight — black backgrounds, warm red figures, feasts that glow out of the dark. He died in a basement on Molokan Street. Today his canvases hang in the National Gallery. His life was a study of red light in the dark.

Our deepest lens is named for him. Wear it in the hours he worked.

### Function (translated from lab data)

- **Transmission (Tv):** 13.07% — the world goes dim.
- **Blocks:** ~99% of blue (400–500 nm), ~90% of green (500–600 nm), 100% of UV-A and UV-B.
- **Passes:** red wavelengths above 600 nm.
- **Net effect:** the wavelengths that suppress melatonin and delay sleep onset are removed; the wavelengths that don't are preserved. You can still read, cook, talk, move — in a room rendered in firelight tones.

### When to wear

- 2–3 hours before bed, under household LEDs or phone screens.
- On evening flights.
- In any bright space after 9 PM.
- Not during driving — fails ANSI Z80.3 traffic-signal tests, as designed. Red lenses are not driving lenses.

### Lab certification (summarized)

| Standard                 | Overall                   | Why                                                        |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| ANSI Z80.3:2018          | Not certified for driving | Intentional — this is a sleep lens, not a traffic lens     |
| EN ISO 12312-1:2022      | Filter Category 3         | Strong tint, appropriate for artificial-light environments |
| UV-A / UV-B              | 100% blocked              | Full UV protection                                         |
| Blue-transmission floors | Below minimums            | **By design** — removing blue is the product               |

> **On the "failures":** the red lens does not meet blue-transmission floors required for general-purpose or traffic glasses. Those standards assume the wearer needs to see all wavelengths. Pirosmani is built to do the opposite — block blue and green to protect your circadian rhythm. The lab fails are the feature.

## 7. 02 · RUSTAVELI 🟡

### Model code

`LMX-02-RUS-YEL`

### One-line

**The work lens.** Long hours at a screen without the fatigue.

### Positioning in one sentence

If Pirosmani protects your sleep, Rustaveli protects your workday. It is the lens for deep work — the hours you spend at a desk, in front of a screen, trying to think clearly.

### The name

**Shota Rustaveli** (12th century) wrote _The Knight in the Panther's Skin_ — ვეფხისტყაოსანი — Georgia's national epic. **1,600 stanzas.** Written by hand, at the court of Queen Tamar, in the Golden Age of Georgia. That kind of output is not a burst of inspiration — it's years of sustained, focused, daylight work. The 12th-century equivalent of what we now call _deep work_.

The language Rustaveli shaped in those 1,600 stanzas became the backbone of literary Georgian. Eight hundred years later, every Georgian child memorizes his lines. His statue stands at the head of Tbilisi's central avenue.

Our work lens is named for him because he did the thing we built this lens for: he sat down, stayed focused for hours, and produced something that lasted. The lens carries his name because it carries his craft — attention, sustained, across a long day.

### Function (translated from lab data)

- **Transmission (Tv):** 79.87% — the world stays bright, no one sees a "tint."
- **UV blocked:** 100% UV-A, 100% UV-B.
- **Blue filtered:** meaningful reduction in the 380–470 nm range — exactly where screens, LEDs, and fluorescent lights emit most heavily.
- **Net effect:** long hours at a screen stop costing you. Eye fatigue drops. Focus holds. Headaches from afternoon LED exposure disappear. You finish the day with energy left.
- **Bonus:** certified for day and night driving (passes ANSI Z80.3 traffic-signal recognition and EN ISO night-driving thresholds). The lens is built for work, but it's road-legal at any hour.

### When to wear

- **At the desk.** Every hour you spend in front of a monitor.
- **Deep work sessions.** Writing, coding, designing, studying — anything that demands sustained focus.
- **Back-to-back meetings on video.** The Zoom/Meet tax on your eyes disappears.
- **Long reading.** Books, documents, screens with dense text.
- **Secondary:** driving (day or night), overcast days outside, fluorescent-lit transit spaces.

### Lab certification (summarized)

| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| ANSI Z80.3:2018          | **Passes** as cosmetic/daily-wear lens     | Full traffic-signal recognition                 |

| AS/NZS 1067.1:2016       | Category 1, passes driving & night-driving | Certified for twilight / night driving          |

> **On the "failure":** the only standard Rustaveli fails is the LED blue-signal-recognition floor — because the lens is built to attenuate blue. Like Pirosmani, it's a feature, not a defect. Every other test passes cleanly, including the one that matters for real life: you can drive in this lens, day or night.
